## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take security issues seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to us privately to protect the project and its users.

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues.**

Instead, please send an email to `security@example.com` with the subject line `Security Vulnerability Report: Test Management Tool`.

### What to Include

Please provide as much information as possible in your report, including:

- **Description**: A clear description of the vulnerability.
- **Impact**: What is the potential impact of this vulnerability?
- **Steps to Reproduce**: Detailed steps to reproduce the vulnerability, including any setup required.
- **Proof of Concept**: A demonstration of the vulnerability (e.g., code, screenshots, or a video).
- **Environment**: The version of the application, browser, OS, and any other relevant details.
- **Contact Information**: Your name and a way to contact you.

### Our Process

1.  **Acknowledgment**: We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours.
2.  **Assessment**: We will investigate the vulnerability to understand its scope and impact.
3.  **Updates**: We will keep you informed of our progress.
4.  **Resolution**: We will work to fix the vulnerability and release a patch.
5.  **Disclosure**: Once the vulnerability is patched, we will publicly disclose the issue, giving you credit for the discovery if you wish.

We aim to resolve critical vulnerabilities within 30 days of the report.

## Security Best Practices

- **Keep dependencies updated**: Use `pnpm audit` to check for vulnerabilities in dependencies.
- **Use strong, unique passwords**: For your database and other services.
- **Secure your environment**: Follow security best practices for your hosting environment.
- **Limit access**: Grant access to the application and its resources on a need-to-know basis.

## Responsible Disclosure

We kindly ask that you follow responsible disclosure principles and not disclose the vulnerability publicly until we have had a chance to address it.

Thank you for helping to keep the Test Management Tool secure!
